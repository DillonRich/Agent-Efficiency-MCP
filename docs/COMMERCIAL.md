## What AGPL means here
- Open forks, contributions, and AGPL-compliant deployments are welcome.
- If you distribute a modified version, or run a modified version as a network service others use, you must offer the corresponding source under AGPL-3.0.
- AGPL does not send invoices. It pushes you to keep derivatives open, or stop using the work under AGPL.

## Closed-source use
If you want this engine (or a fork) inside a closed product, internal proprietary stack, or commercial SaaS without meeting AGPL share-alike, you need a separate commercial license from the copyright holder.

That is dual licensing. AGPL covers community use. Proprietary rights are a paid agreement.

AGPL-compliant use does not require contacting us.
