## What AGPL means for companies
- Open community forks, contributions, and AGPL-compliant deployments are welcome.
- If someone **distributes** a modified version, or runs a **modified** version as a network service that others interact with, they must offer the corresponding source under AGPL-3.0 (“share alike”).
- AGPL does **not** automatically invoice anyone. It creates a strong incentive: keep the derivative open, or stop using it under AGPL.

## Closed-source / monetary use
If a company wants to embed this engine (or a fork) in a **closed product, internal proprietary stack, or commercial SaaS** without open-sourcing their modifications / combined work as AGPL requires, they need a **separate commercial license** from the copyright holder.

That is how licensing fees or revenue share work: not via AGPL itself, but via **dual licensing** — they contact us and we grant proprietary rights under a paid agreement.

Community / AGPL-compliant use does **not** require contacting us.
